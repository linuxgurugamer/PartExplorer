Browse • Inspect • Filter • Compare

PartExplorer is an utility mod for Kerbal Space Program 1, which allows you to browse, filter, inspect and compare the parts installed in your game; it displays the loaded part database in a searchable and sortable table with thumbnails, filters by mods and categories, integration with the editor filter, detailed information about the parts and rotating preview of the parts. It can display information about the stock modules, resources, science information, SCANsat data and configurable information about the supported science mods.

You can pick the parts for comparison, with the possibility of hiding the identical rows, highlighting the differences, marking the low/high numeric values, and displaying the deltas. The PartExplorer application also has customizable Details and Compare panes, summary of the loaded mods, inserting the parts into the VAB/SPH, right click actions to insert parts into the editor/comparison list, copy part IDs or paths. The application is intended to be useful both when playing the game and when developing/troubleshooting your modded installation.
